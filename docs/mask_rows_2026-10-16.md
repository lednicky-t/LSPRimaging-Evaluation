# Mask rows under the Cube and wavelength sliders (2026-10-16)

Status: **built** (uncommitted when written). Engineering priority served: 1 (the user sees at a glance which cubes / wavelengths use a mask of their own, so a result is not read against the wrong mask) and 2 (a hand-drawn mask can no longer be lost by one click on a tiny mark: the row commands are undoable). Sister of `roi_layout_row_2026-10-16.md` (the ROIs row, the zoom, the stepping); everything there that is not repeated here is shared.

## 1. What the maintainer asked for

"Something similar for mask, but since a mask can also be for a wavelength, two sliders: one under the Cube slider and one under the wavelength slider. Everything else pretty similar to ROI; no selection control, as there is only one ignore mask. The slider will be called Mask; the ROI slider is renamed ROIs."

Answers to the open questions: (1) a cube with only a persistent mask shows the `>` of the ROIs row, only individual masks a `|`, **both a filled `>`**; (2) **undo may be added**.

## 2. Decisions

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Data | Unchanged: `MaskModule`'s timeline (persistent masks keyed by cube, "this cube and every cube after"; individual masks keyed by the exact (cube, wavelength) frame). No format change, no migration. |
| One kind per cube | **Does not carry over from the ROIs row.** Several different individual masks of one cube (one per wavelength) cannot merge into one persistent mask, and "a persistent mask for the cube plus a fix for one wavelength" is a natural workflow. A cube may hold both; the row shows it as the filled `>`. |
| Cube row | A mark at every cube with a mask of its own: `>` persistent (drawn at some wavelength, applies to the whole cube and on), `\|` individual (masks of single wavelengths), filled `>` both. **No `>` at the first cube by default**: unlike the ROIs there is no start layout, nothing is masked until a mask is drawn. |
| Wavelength row | The wavelengths of the cube shown that have an individual mask (thin lines). A persistent mask belongs to the whole cube, so it is on the Cube row only. Refreshed on every cube step. |
| Names / order | Row titles "ROIs" (was "ROI") and "Mask" (both rows). Order: Cube, ROIs, Mask, wavelength, Mask. |
| Colour | **No colour control** (like the ROIs: the rows are sliders of their own). The Mask swatch and the "Slider colors" ribbon section are gone; the marks are a fixed orange (`mask_rows.MASK_COLOR`; green, red, purple, blue and gold are taken). A saved `image/slider_color_mask` / `_roi` is ignored. |
| Ribbon | New "Mask" section: one toggle that shows / hides both rows (remembered: `image/mask_rows_visible`). |
| Undo | **Yes, for the rows' commands only** (delete / make individual / make persistent). Painting, loading and clearing stay non-undoable (they would need stroke batching and a memory decision: 200 undo entries x full-image arrays). `CLAUDE.md` of the app is updated. |
| Stepping | The slideshow's "step through" choice has a fourth entry, **Mask states** (cube + mask icon): Previous / Next / Play hop between the Cube row's marks on the wavelength shown now. The wheel over the Cube row does the same; the wheel over the wavelength row hops between the wavelengths with a mask in the cube shown. |
| Zoom | Each row shares its slider's `AxisWindow` (Cube row with the Cube slider, wavelength row with the λ slider): free from step 4 of the ROIs work. |

Decided by me, on the maintainer's silence, and easy to change: the orange, removing the Mask swatch, the Mask-states stepping mode, the greyed "Make persistent" when the cube already has its own persistent mask (it would overwrite), the greyed "Make individual" when that frame already has an individual mask.

## 3. The undo design (the part to keep in mind)

Row commands are undoable, painting is not. A plain undo entry could then overwrite a mask drawn after it ("undo delete" restoring the old persistent mask over a newer stroke). So each command carries a **version token**:

- `MaskModule._version` identifies the state of the timeline. Every write that is *not* an undoable row command (`set_mask_change`, `clear_all_masks`, `restore_state`) takes a fresh token (`_touch`).
- A row command remembers the token before and after it. Its **undo is applied only while the timeline still has the token the command left; its redo only while it has the token the command found.** Otherwise nothing is touched and `MaskModule.undo_skipped(str)` says why; the Image panel shows it in the status line.
- Consecutive row commands undo and redo in order; a stroke in between protects only the commands before it.
- Cost: the undo entries share the (never modified in place) mask arrays; nothing is copied.

**Right-click menus** (all undoable, all with a sentence in the status line):
- Cube row, persistent mask: Go to this cube, Delete the persistent mask (drawn at X nm), Make it individual (X nm only: the cubes after it go back to the persistent mask before, or to no mask).
- Cube row, individual masks: Delete the individual masks of this cube (N wavelengths: ... nm).
- Cube row, both: all of the above.
- Wavelength row: Go to X nm, Make it the persistent mask of this cube (greyed if the cube already has its own), Delete this mask.

## 5. Cost

The Cube row is rebuilt when the mask changes (coalesced 100 ms) or the dataset does, never per cube step (only the hairline moves). The wavelength row is refreshed on every cube step: O(wavelengths of that cube) plus one dict lookup for the individual masks of the cube. `state_cubes()` (Mask states mode, per Play step) is O(persistent + individual cubes), which are the user's drawn masks (tens).
